Where will the centre bracket fix?
Side brackets are always wall-mounted. This is the centre bracket only.
📐 Ceiling mount — no wall gap needed. Measure along the bay walls (not the window frame). If the frame runs to the ceiling with no wall above, measure below the window sill — the bay width is identical at any height.
Bracket reach
The bracket reach is the distance the pole sits out from the wall or ceiling. You don't need to factor this into your measurements — just measure the bay walls and we apply the offset when building. Choose a longer reach if you have a deep window trim, radiator or other obstruction.
📐 Brackets need a minimum 75 mm of flat wall or timber surface to fix to — this applies to the wall above the window, the angled bay walls, and any ceiling fixing point.
⚠️ Custom bracket reach needs to be agreed before we build — we’ll be in touch to confirm measurements and confirm the extra cost.

Who will measure the bay? *
Measure along the bay walls — not the window frame. The pole follows the wall line, so your tape should run flat against each wall of the bay.
🧰 You'll need: tape measure · stepladder (pole is fitted near ceiling height) · a second person to hold the tape end. Most bay measurements really do need two people.
📏 Measuring depth: stretch a string line or second tape measure straight across the front opening of the bay (wall-to-wall). Then measure from that line back to the front bay wall — that's your depth. If it's a ceiling bay, you can use the ceiling line as your reference instead.
