2 Pole Setup
Does the pole extend onto the front wall?
Recommended — lets curtains clear the window fully. Minimum 30 cm each side needed for the pole to return onto the wall.
Available wall space each side (cm)
The pole itself will overhang the wall by ~25 cm + finial — this is separate from the wall clearance above. Just tell us the available space.
Is there a wall or timber head above the window?
The centre bracket mounts on the head above the window — we need at least 75 mm of flat wall or timber there. Block is ideal but timber frame works too. If the window goes right to the ceiling with nothing above, we fix to the bay ceiling instead.
📐 Ceiling mount — no wall gap needed. Measure along the bay walls (not the window frame). If the frame runs to the ceiling with no wall above, measure below the window sill — the bay width is identical at any height.
Bracket reach
Need extended reach?
Choose Extended (120 mm) if you have a deep window trim, radiator, or other obstruction the pole needs to clear. Custom if you need something specific — we’ll confirm cost before building.
⚠️ Custom bracket reach needs to be agreed before we build — we’ll be in touch to confirm measurements and confirm the extra cost.
